The recent decisions to remove liability or obligation to actually deliver packages on the part of logistics companies in the US is a already having some unsavory consequences.

### Come on, guys, seriously?

I live in a gated building with a door code. Whenever I place a delivery, I enter the door code. If I prompted by the carrier for more delivery information (re: not having a door code), I update it. Easy peasy. Except when the next delivery date gets the exact same reply: no delivery code. But, also, you were literally there all day waiting for the delivery. There is a call box. It never buzzed. And then when I updated my delivery information a third time, the package stopped receiving updates. Sadly, I had already placed another order that used this same carrier... Bam. Failed first delivery attempt. No code. Fuck this, I decide, damn the horrid inconvenience of not having a car and having to pick up my delivery, I need the package so I attempt to having them hold it for pickup... Only to find they now charge $7.99 for that. This feels a lot like being extorted: I already paid for services but you won't actually render them unless I pay you even more and inconvenience myself? Sweet. Cool. Thanks. Fuck you, UPS. Fuck right off.
